About me and my gap year

My name is Carl. I graduated from the University of Toronto last month (June 2026) with a degree in engineering. At the crossroad where some of my peers are entering into their careers and others are pursuing further studies, I decide to slow down a bit. I wish to take a gap year to identify what my priorities are and what I truly want to accomplish. I tell my friends that I will be farming this year. I describe to them – with much passion and imagery – that I will be living in a farmhouse, waking up at dawn to graze sheep, milk cows, and contemplate life. Farm life appeals to me because I have never experienced anything like that growing up in big cities. Having lived in condo apartments all my life, bustling city centres are my comfort zone. Thus, the idea of farming and getting out of the city serves better as an analogy of me wanting to challenge myself and step out of my comfort zone. I have identified several areas that I wish to explore: literature, photography, and learning about different cultures around the globe. My blog will be covering these areas. However, the list may expand, because on a farm, the sky is the limit!
