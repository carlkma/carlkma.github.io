Why am I starting a blog in 2026?

Choosing to start a blog is a conscious decision after defining my goal and evaluating the different media and platforms available to me. My goal is to document the things I did in my gap year along with my reflections. Why is that important? Ask a student what they did last December, they may tell you right away that they were studying for the final exams. Ask an accountant what they did last April, they may tell you that they were preparing tax filings. A gap year is different because there may not be clear deadlines and milestones. Without documentation, I fear that my accomplishments this year will become blurry when I look back. I fear that my gap year lives like an extended holiday in my memory, as opposed to a period of personal growth that I envisioned. A private journal works well for this goal, but I want to make it public for two reasons. First, I favour discussion and hope that my thoughts could be critiqued. I welcome any input from friends and strangers. I am eager to hear if they agree or disagree with my interpretation of things. The second reason to make my document public is perhaps a more ambitious one. I understand that not everyone has the privilege to take a gap year. I wish my writing could take people on a virtual trip that introduces fresh perspectives, ignites thinking, and fuels their own development in thought and character. I do not expect a lot of readers on my blog. If I do, I would have started a TikTok or Instagram account, filling it with the latest catchphrases and trends. Instead, I prefer a more formal blog. Less viewers for sure, but less noise, less distractions. The audience of this blog is those who actively click into my page – those who value knowledge, reflection, and intellectual discussion.
